# References: Elastic Wire Knots & Related

## Key Paper

**Computational Exploration of Multistable Elastic Knots**
Vidulis, Ren, Panetta, Grinspun, Pauly — SIGGRAPH / ACM Transactions on Graphics, 2023
Simulates wires with bending resistance settling into equilibrium shapes; finds many knot
topologies have multiple stable states; ran on thousands of knot types.
- Paper: https://www.julianpanetta.com/pdf/knots.pdf
- Code (open source): https://github.com/EPFL-LGG/ElasticKnots
- Project page: https://www.epfl.ch/labs/gcm/research-projects/elastic-knots/
- Lay summary: https://phys.org/news/2023-09-team-thousands.html

## Physics Framework

**Discrete Elastic Rods**
Bergou et al. — Columbia University (computer graphics, more accessible than pure math)
The standard simulation framework for wire/rod physics (bending + twisting stiffness).
- Paper: https://www.cs.columbia.edu/cg/pdfs/143-rods.pdf
- ACM page: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1360612.1360662

## Turk's Head Knot Mathematics

**Turk's Head Knots and Links: A Survey**
Di Prisa & Şavk — arXiv, 2024
Comprehensive mathematical survey of Th(p,q) knots; classification, invariants, open problems.
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.20106

**A one-variable bracket polynomial for some Turk's head knots**
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.05256
